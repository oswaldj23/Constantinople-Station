# Privacy Policy — Constantinople (shop)

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Constantinople is a small print-on-demand shop. This policy covers the shop's own tools, including the software that posts our listings to Pinterest and other social platforms.

**What we collect.** Our tools act only on our own accounts (our Etsy shop, our Pinterest profile, our YouTube channel). They do not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors, customers, or other users of those platforms. Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy under Etsy's privacy policy; we see only what Etsy shows every seller (order details needed to fulfil an order).

**Cookies and tracking.** Our tools set no cookies and run no tracking.

**Third parties.** Orders are produced and shipped by our print partner (Printify and its printers), who receive the shipping details needed to deliver an order. Nothing else is shared or sold.

**Your rights.** For anything about an order or your data, message the shop on Etsy and we will respond.
